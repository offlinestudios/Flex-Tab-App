Native privacy audit — draft, not submitted

Evidence reviewed: ios/App/App/Info.plist; CapApp-SPM/Package.swift;
Capacitor 8.5.3 and bundled Cordova PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy; Capacitor App/Browser
Swift sources; client/src/components/CardioGPSTracker.tsx; media/profile upload
flows; drizzle/schema.ts; client HTML/TS and server TS searches for analytics SDKs.

Purpose descriptions added for user-initiated foreground GPS workout tracking,
camera capture, video microphone capture and selected photo/video upload. No
background location entitlement or always-location request is added. Actual
WKWebView prompts, permission denial/retry and capture behavior require physical
iPhone testing. Modern system photo pickers may not request whole-library access.
Do not add broad library permissions or background modes unless required by tests.

Known data flows to reconcile with App Store Connect privacy answers:
- Account name/email and internal/auth user IDs: authentication and account display.
- Sets, reps, weights, exercises, workout history and goals: fitness functionality.
- Body weight/body measurements: health/fitness functionality; linked to account.
- Route coordinates/polyline: precise location, saved with GPS activities.
- Profile photos, post photos/videos, captions, comments, bio and reports: user content.
- Moderation/deletion records: account operations; see retention/retry documentation.
These are linked to accounts and transmitted to application/backend providers.
Optional user entry does not by itself remove disclosure requirements.

Native dependency inventory: Capacitor 8.5.3, Cordova bridge, Capacitor App 8.1.2,
Capacitor Browser 8.0.5. Capacitor/Cordova ship privacy manifests. The inspected
Capacitor manifest declares no accessed-reason APIs or collected data; this does
not describe FlexTab's own backend collection. No direct required-reason API use
was found by the limited app/App/Browser Swift-source search. Archive inspection
and Xcode's consolidated privacy report remain required; source searches are not
proof that every transitive SDK is covered.

Web/service inventory: Supabase auth/storage, Railway app/PostgreSQL, optional
Cloudflare R2, Google Maps for route display, external OAuth provider. No dedicated
analytics/ad SDK references found in the targeted client/server source search.
Do not treat that as proof of no provider logging/tracking. Inspect external scripts,
network requests, backend logs, provider retention, and all production configuration.
Do not submit 'data not collected'. Do not invent required-reason codes.

Release gates: produce and inspect final signed archive, confirm manifests in all
embedded SDKs, review Xcode privacy report, reconcile policy and App Store Connect
answers with actual production collection/retention, verify location/media consent
on a physical device, and review Google Maps/provider terms and logging.
